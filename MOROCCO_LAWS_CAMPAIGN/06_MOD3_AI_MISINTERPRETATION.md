# 06 — MOD 3: PREVENTING AI MISINTERPRETATION OF HUMAN INTENT IN COMPLEX CONFLICT ENVIRONMENTS
**Core thesis:** machines already misread reality at machine speed, and humans rubber-stamp it. This is documented, fatal, and — most importantly — *designable-against* by treaty. Morocco converts failure case-law into binding engineering.

## WHY MOROCCO OWNS THIS MOD

Mod 3 is where the agenda stops being theoretical. It is also where Morocco's posture is strongest: we are not arguing against technology — **we are arguing for the engineering conditions under which technology may carry lethal power.** That is a moderate, unanswerable, leadable position.

## THE EVIDENCE BANK (verified, dated, labelled)

### CASE A — Patriot fratricides, Iraq, 2003: automation bias kills allies
- `[VERIFIED FACT]` Over nine days of OIF, the US Army's Patriot system was involved in **two fratricides**: a **British Tornado GR4** and a **US Navy F/A-18**, killing **three aircrew** (CNAS "Patriot Wars," Scharre; Cummings, JOTS v32).
- The system misclassified friendly aircraft as missile tracks; displays were "confusing and often incorrect"; operators had about a **ten-second veto** window and over-trusted the machine — **automation misuse/bias** identified as the major contributor.
- After the first fratricide, units switched to a "second look" regimen — and the **second fratricide happened under that modified regimen**, when "bring launchers to ready" effectively ordered an automatic engagement.
- **Committee translation:** this was *pre-AI* automation, operated by the best-resourced military on earth, and it still killed allies. *"Intent" was never the machine's category — geometry was.* A false radar track was processed as an anti-radiation missile; the human chain ratified it.

### CASE B — "Lavender / The Gospel / Where's Daddy," Gaza, 2023–24: decision-support as de-facto target selection
- `[CONTESTED CLAIM — reported by +972 Magazine/Local Call, 3 Apr 2024, disputed by the IDF]` Lavender scored Gaza residents for suspected militant affiliation — reportedly **37,000 people** marked at peak; sources described a **~10% error rate**; training criteria problematically broad (WhatsApp groups, address changes; inclusion of civil-defence employees in training data).
- Human review reportedly functioned as a **"rubber stamp" — about 20 seconds per target**, often only to check the mark was male; "Where's Daddy" tracked marked persons to their homes.
- IDF response: the systems are "merely tools/databases," and "analysts must conduct independent examinations" — *we cite both sides, always.*
- **Committee translation (safe):** *whatever the truth of the details*, the case proves the structural failure mode: when a system generates targets at industrial scale showing 90% confidence, **nominal human-in-the-loop degrades into automation bias with a signature.**

### CASE C — Drone-war operational pressure, Ukraine, 2022–26 (context)
- `[CONTEXTUAL FACT]` Mass FPV/loitering-munition warfare; reports of AI-assisted terminal guidance appearing in 2024–25; the June 2025 "Spider Web" operation used **semi-autonomous, AI-enhanced** drones under remote human control (file 07).
- Reading: the direction of travel is shortening human roles to survive jamming — **the delegation pressure to cut the human out is operational, not hypothetical.**

### CASE D — The technical failure taxonomy (stable knowledge) `[CONTEXTUAL FACT]`
- **Automation bias:** humans defer to machine outputs under time pressure (Patriot; aviation research).
- **Dataset/label bias:** training data encodes wrongful proxies (Lavender criteria; civilian men flagged as combatants by pattern — flagged in GGE research papers on gender/race bias).
- **Distribution shift:** models trained in sandbox meet unrepresentative environments (desert vs. urban; camouflage; decoys).
- **Adversarial spoofing:** cheap decoys/jamming inducing false tracks (Ukraine ECM war; Patriot-type false tracks).
- **Interface confusion:** "ready" meaning "fire" (Patriot F/A-18 case) — human factors are legal factors.
- **Semantic gap:** machines infer *signatures*, not *intent* — a pickup carrying a coffin and a pickup carrying a mortar are the same geometry.

Failure taxonomy → specific IHL duties it threatens → treaty mechanism that answers it:
