# 07 — MOD 4: ALGORITHMIC ATTRIBUTION & STATE RESPONSIBILITY IN CROSS-BORDER "SWARM" ENCOUNTERS
**Core thesis:** swarms break 20th-century attribution. When a hundred munitions launch from a container ship, a subcontractor's van, or a hijacked civilian network, "who did it?" becomes a legal problem *before* it becomes a military one. Morocco writes the attribution chapter.

## THE CASE FILES (verified; each teaches a different legal lesson)

### CASE 1 — Abqaiq & Khurais, 14 Sep 2019: the attribution dispute that changed nothing
- `[VERIFIED FACT]` ~18 UAS + 7 cruise missiles (≈25 weapons) struck Saudi Aramco's Abqaiq plant and Khurais field, knocking out **~5% of global oil supply**; advanced air defences failed.
- `[VERIFIED FACT]` Houthis claimed responsibility; a **UN investigation determined the attack did NOT originate from Yemen** and that Houthis lacked the capability to build the systems; the US and KSA blamed **Iran; Iran denied**. (UN Panel findings as reported; DTIC thesis compilation, 2019–20.)
- **Lesson:** a proxy claim + deniable supply chain = **perfect responsibility laundering**. No legal mechanism forced clarity; the world moved on. *This is the swarm era's founding precedent — and it is a failure precedent.*

### CASE 2 — Khmeimim airbase, Syria, Jan 2018: the non-state swarm
- `[CONTEXTUAL FACT — widely reported; mark for verification]` ~13 improvised drones in a coordinated attack on Russia's Syrian base; defeated by EW/air defence;attributed to non-state actors with external technical input.
- **Lesson:** swarms are **cheap enough for militias**. Attribution to a *state sponsor* becomes the operative question (ARSIWA Art. 8 control standards; due diligence).

### CASE 3 — Perdix, Oct 2016: the state R&D benchmark
- `[VERIFIED FACT]` US Strategic Capabilities Office released **103 Perdix micro-drones from 3 F/A-18s** over China Lake; distributed "swarm brain," collective formation, adaptive mission roles (DoD press reporting; Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists).
- **Lesson:** military-grade swarms are not prototypes anymore — they are fielded programs in multiple states (plus loitering-munition exports: Harop, Kargu-2, Lancet...). Cephalopod: the time to write rules is before proliferation, not after.

### CASE 4 — Libya, Mar 2020: autonomy on African soil
- `[VERIFIED FACT]` UN Panel of Experts (S/2021/229): Kargu-2 loitering munitions — *"a true 'fire, forget and find' capability"* — hunted logistics convoys and retreating fighters; "programmed to attack targets **without requiring data connectivity between the operator and the munition**."
- Nuance (always state it): casualties unconfirmed; manufacturer STM claims man-in-the-loop doctrine `[CONTESTED points of fact]`.
- **Lesson:** first **documented battlefield report** of autonomous engagement — on the African continent. Morocco's regional standing to speak is unique.

### CASE 5 — Operation Spider Web, 1 Jun 2025: cross-border swarm logistics, cleanly attributed
- `[VERIFIED FACT]` Ukraine's SBU smuggled **117 FPV drones** in trucks deep into Russia; launched near four-five airbases; damaged/destroyed **41 aircraft** claimed (~$7B), incl. strategic bombers; AI-enhanced terminal guidance with remote human control over 4G/LTE; SBU claimed responsibility.
- **Lesson:** "border" means nothing to pre-positioned swarms; but *attribution was easy because a state owned the act.* Next time, no one may claim it. Our law must be built for the **denied case**, not the clean one.

## THE LEGAL TOOLBOX (Mod 4's spine) `[CONTEXTUAL FACT — doctrine; verify L-04]`

**ARSIWA (2001)** — the attribution grammar states already accept:
- **Art. 4:** conduct of state organs → attributable. (State-built swarm? Easy.)
- **Art. 5:** entities empowered to exercise governmental authority. (State-owned defence contractors? Arguable.)
- **Art. 8:** private conduct attributable if instructions, direction, or *effective control* (Nicaragua) / *overall control* for organized groups (Tadić). **Swarm problem:** is "sold the targeting model + spare parts" enough? Courts~lag tech.
- **Art. 2:** wrongful act = conduct attributable + breach. Jus ad bellum (Charter 2(4))/IHL breaches plug in here.
- **Due diligence (corfu-channel lineage + cyber doctrine analogies):** states must prevent territory being used to harm other states — spider-web logistics make this the operative duty for launch-from-territory cases.
- **Countermeasures & self-defence:** Art. 51 responses to swarm attacks below "armed attack" thresholds — the escalation trap Morocco flags.

**The five swarm-shaped holes in that grammar** (our mod's attack surface):
1. **Software provenance gap:** the swarm's "conduct" sits in a model trained in one state, sold by a second, launched from a third state's territory by a fourth state's proxies.
2. **Deniability economics:** small UAS + commercial parts = mass-produced anonymity (Abqaiq proved it works).
3. **Speed asymmetry:** attribution processes take months; swarms act in minutes → states face pressure to "respond first, attribute later" (escalation risk).
4. **Human-trace dissolution:** when no human selected the target, which human's conduct do we even attribute?
5. **Threshold fog:** cumulative micro-strikes (each below "armed attack") evading Article 51 logic.

## MOROCCO'S LEADABLE MECHANISMS (FAMMS-checked)

1. **Lifecycle Provenance & Logging Duty** — every regulated AWS keeps tamper-evident **operation logs** (activation, targeting events, human interventions, engagement count) transferable to a **UN repository on demand in international investigations**. *(Pilot: CCW HCPs; fund via IHL capacity trust.)*
2. **Chain-of-Custody in Model & Parts Trade** — export-control-style documentation for targeting models and swarm-critical components (Wassenaar-adjacent; no tech transfer bans — *traceability, not prohibition*).
3. **Presumption of Responsibility for Systems Deployed** — a state that develops, fields, or transfers an AWS **retains responsibility** for its employment absent rebuttal proof (codifying Box V's "states remain responsible" into an evidentiary rule).
4. **UN Technical Attribution Roster** — standing panel of forensic experts (UNIDIR/UNODA-supported) able to deploy within 30 days to examine debris, datalink records, and model artefacts; **30-day clock answers the speed asymmetry**.
5. **Incident Notification Protocol** — mandatory notification of autonomous-engagement incidents crossing borders to the Security Council *and* affected states within fixed hours/manageable scope (confidence measure, mirrors INCSEA philosophy).
6. **Due-Diligence Codification for Territory** — duty to prevent pre-positioned swarm launches from one's territory, with capacity support so poorer states can actually comply (Morocco's Global South design principle: **no obligation without capability support**).
