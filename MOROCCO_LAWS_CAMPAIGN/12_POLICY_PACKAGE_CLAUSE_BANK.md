## THE RABAT FRAMEWORK FOR HUMAN CONTROL OVER THE USE OF FORCE
*A two-tier binding instrument architecture: PROHIBIT the unacceptable, REGULATE the rest, ATTRIBUTE every engagement, EQUIP every state.*

### PILLAR 1 — Characterization (Mod 1 payoff)
> *"For the purposes of this instrument, an autonomous weapon system is a functionally integrated combination of one or more weapons and technological components, including components distributed across networks, that can identify, select, and engage a target without intervention by a human operator in the execution of those tasks. A system does not escape this characterization by reason of a human setting target parameters, nor by use against objects rather than persons."*
- **Rationale:** rolling-text spine + distributed-architecture fix + China-neutral (no "lethal" sidetrack).
- **Defensive carve-out clause (narrow):** *"…excluding fixed or platform-mounted defensive counter-materiel systems whose engagement parameters are set by a human operator, operate within bounded spatial-temporal envelopes, and cannot select persons as targets."*

### PILLAR 2 — Prohibitions (Tier 1) (Mods 2+3 payoff)
> *"Each State Party undertakes never under any circumstances to develop, produce, acquire, stockpile, transfer, or use:*
> *(a) autonomous weapon systems designed or used to select and engage persons as targets;*
> *(b) autonomous weapon systems whose effects cannot be sufficiently understood, predicted, and explained to permit their use in compliance with international humanitarian law;*
> *(c) autonomous weapon systems incapable of operation with context-appropriate human judgement and control."*
- Anti-personnel ban = our moral headline (SG's "moral line," ICRC rec #2, Morocco's 2013 DNA).
- Accept **phase fork** for swing states: 2(a) immediate; 2(b)–(c) entry into force +24 months with review conference.

### PILLAR 3 — Regulation of permitted systems (Tier 2)
> *"Prior to any employment of an autonomous weapon system not prohibited under Article [2], a State Party shall ensure: (i) a documented legal review …; (ii) testing in realistic operational environments, including against adaptive countermeasures; (iii) defined limits on target types, duration, geographic scope, scale, and the number of autonomous engagements per human authorization; (iv) human-machine interaction enabling timely intervention and deactivation; (v) re-validation following any modification affecting targeting functions."*
- Mirrors DoDD 3000.09 practice → US can't call it radical. 6D(ii)-style engagement limits defended.

### PILLAR 4 — Transparency, logging & attribution (Mod 4 payoff)
> *"(i) Operation logs recording activation, targeting events, human interventions, and engagement counts shall be generated and preserved in tamper-evident form; (ii) logs shall be made available to a [UN technical roster] upon request in connection with investigations of cross-border incidents; (iii) States Parties transferring such systems shall document chain-of-custody of targeting models and swarm-critical components; (iv) a State Party that develops, fields, or transfers a system retains responsibility for its employment unless rebutted by credible evidence; (v) notification of cross-border autonomous engagements [within 72 hours] to affected states and the Security Council."*
- This is **Morocco's signature pillar** — nobody else in the room owns attribution; we do.

### PILLAR 5 — Accountability
> *"States Parties affirm that international law imposes obligations on States, parties to armed conflict, and individuals, and not on machines; that human responsibility for decisions on the use of force shall be traceable through a documented chain of command; and that each State Party shall maintain criminal jurisdiction over grave breaches committed through the use of autonomous weapon systems."*
- Direct descendent of GGE Box V — consensus-ready.

### PILLAR 6 — Implementation & capacity equity (the Global-South closer)
> *"(i) A voluntary trust fund shall support Article 36-type legal review capacity, testing methodology, and log infrastructure in developing States Parties; (ii) States Parties shall exchange best practices on weapons reviews annually, without disclosure of classified material; (iii) a Review Conference shall convene [24] months after entry into force to consider expansion of prohibitions and the state of technology."*
- Morocco's convening offer: **host a North-Africa/African Group review-capacity workshop** — puts our flag on the map and buys African votes.
- FAMMS: F (rolling-text-anchored), A (CCW + UNGA mandates), M (logs, reviews, reporting), M (trust fund), S (sovereignty-preserving: no classified disclosure; capacity-first).

## WORKING PAPER → RESOLUTION ROADMAP `[NEGOTIATION STRATEGY]`

**Caucus phase:** WP 1.0 "Elements for a binding instrument" — co-sponsored Brazil (lead), Morocco, Ireland, Sri Lanka, Portugal, Italy, Cuba, Venezuela. Structure shadowing the 5 rolling-text boxes + our Pillars 4 & 6 as additive annexes. Never compete with the rolling text; *complete* it.

**Draft resolution (DISEC-style) operative core:**
1. *Welcomes* the GGE rolling text… (OP1, harmless, buys consensus smell)
2. *Calls upon* the 7th Review Conference to adopt a mandate to negotiate a legally binding instrument on the two-tier basis… (OP2 — THE ask)
3. *Requests* the Secretary-General to convene preparatory consultations on negotiating architecture, reporting to GA82… (OP3 — no-months-lost clause)
4. *Decides* to include the item in the provisional agenda of GA82 and take stock in light of the RevCon outcome… (OP4 — the credible UNGA fallback)
5. *Encourages* states to implement Political-Declaration-type measures and report them… (OP5 — gives US-lane states something to join)

**Amendment forks pre-planned:**
- If "legally binding" must be softened to pass: accept "an instrument, which may include legally binding elements" in OP2 — while keeping OP4 UNGA stock-take (the preserve-pressure clause). Fight to the death for OP4.
- If anti-personnel prohibition "divides the room": move it from preambular "recognizing" tier instead of operative — unacceptable as final position; counter-offer a review-conference fast-track clause instead. Never fully surrender the anti-personnel anchor — it's Morocco's soul in this file.
