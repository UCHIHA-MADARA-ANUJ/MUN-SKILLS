# 15 — POI WAR PACK (matrix-specific) + INCOMING-FIRE PROTECTION
**Conference rule exploited:** GSL POIs only on leftover time → fill your 90s, starve enemy questions. In mods, POIs come from delegates who choose to speak — expect them after YOUR 60s. Drills below answer both.
**Doctrine:** hostile POIs are surgical, single-clause, answerable-with-a-number. Friendly POIs are passes that let allies extend the bloc narrative.

## A. OFFENSIVE POIs — FIRE AT THE BLOCKERS

| # | Target | The POI (say exactly) | What it forces |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **United States** | "Your Directive 3000.09 mandates realistic-environment testing, operator intervention, and termination on unintended behaviour. Does the delegate agree these safeguards are necessary — and if so, which precise word in them makes them unacceptable as obligations for other states?" | Trap: either they endorse our clause set, or they admit a double standard |
| P2 | **United States** | "You say a binding instrument is premature. In 1995 this system banned blinding lasers before a single soldier was blinded. Why was prevention heroic in 1995 but premature in 2026?" | Protocol IV precedent — they have no answer |

| P5 | **India** | "Your delegation champions the Global South. If only about a dozen of 174 states can perform Article 36 reviews, does 'existing law suffices' mean law is sufficient for the wealthy and theoretical for everyone else?" | Flips their foreign-policy brand against their obstruction |

| P7 | **Türkiye** | "As a leading drone exporter, would Türkiye gain or lose market legitimacy if its systems carried an internationally certified human-control standard — and will the delegate co-draft Tier-2 operator standards with us?" | Converts an exporter into a co-architect (or exposes pure self-interest) |

## C. DEFENSIVE DRILLS — WHAT COMES BACK AT MOROCCO

**D1 — After GSL (if any time leaks): "Morocco flies Harops. Hypocrite?"**
> "Fifteen seconds, Chair. Morocco flies human-commanded systems — pilots, datalinks, human authorisation of force. Our framework regulates functions, not flags: if any Moroccan system ever selected a person without human intervention, this treaty binds us first. We write the rules for the skies we fly in. That is credibility, not contradiction." *(Do not add more. Sit down metaphorically.)*

**D2 — Algeria fires the Nov 2021 strike allegation:**
> "Chair, that allegation was never independently verified, and this committee will not litigate contested claims. What Morocco offers is exactly what such moments demand: logging, attribution, accountability — written into law for everyone. If Algeria wants verifiable accountability, our working paper has a clause and a chair waiting." *(Never repeat details. Never say "truckers". Bridge in ≤20s.)*

**D3 — "You signed the US Declaration — soft law man in a hard-law costume?"**
> "We signed the stairs and we demand the door. Morocco voted for every resolution on this file — 2023, 2024, 2025. Show me the delegation in this room with a cleaner thirteen-year record, and I will co-sponsor with them." *(Nobody has it. Move on.)*

**D4 — "Two-tier is a license for YOUR drones."**
> "The tiers bind behaviour, not passports. Minimum human judgement, engagement limits, logs — they govern Rabat exactly as they govern every capital here. That is what makes it law rather than favouritism."

**D5 — "Why threaten the CCW with a UNGA track?"**
> "No threat — a deadline. The CCW's own mandate expires in November. 152, then 166, then 156 states did not threaten the Convention; they reminded it the world exists outside its hallway."

## D. POI ECONOMY RULES (how top delegates use questions)

1. **One question = one clause = one number.** If your POI needs a second breath, it's a speech; the chair will cut it and you'll look procedural, not sharp.
2. **Ask, then vanish.** Never debate their answer; the trap is set for the room and judges, not for the speaker. Except: one permitted follow-up — *"The delegate did not name the instrument."* — then silence.
3. **Track who dodges.** Every unanswered P1–P8 becomes a speech line later: *"Three delegations were asked to name the instrument. None could."*
4. **POIs are recruiting:** the friendly passes (F-series) are how you build the visible majority before lunch.
