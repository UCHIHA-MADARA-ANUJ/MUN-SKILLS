## 4. THE FOUR-LAYER POSITION ARCHITECTURE

### Core objectives (must achieve)
1. Launch mandate for a **legally binding instrument on a two-tier architecture** (prohibit the unacceptable, regulate the rest) — anchored on the GGE rolling text as the negotiation basis.
2. A **workable characterization/definition** that captures systems that select and engage targets without human intervention — without swallowing human-supervised systems.
3. Binding obligation for **legal reviews (Article 36-type) with transparency/exchange of practices** + capacity support for developing states.
4. **Human accountability written into law** ("IHL binds states, parties and individuals — not machines").
5. Prohibition of **anti-personnel autonomous targeting** as the moral red line (SG: "a moral line that must not be crossed") — Morocco co-owns this phrase politically.

### Red lines (oppose / heavily condition)
- Any **blanket ban on "autonomous weapons" without tiering** — kills responsible systems, unsellable to sponsors, and unenforceable.
- Any instrument **lacking human-control requirements** (worse than nothing — legitimizes rubber-stamping).
- Any process that **excludes non-military-tech states from drafting** (equal participation principle from A/79/88).
- Language that lets a handful of military powers **veto humanity by consensus rule** forever (support UNGA fallback track).

### Conditional support (accept with safeguards)
- CCW-first route — **conditional**: CCW gets until Nov 2026 RevCon to deliver a negotiation mandate; after that, UNGA-led track is legitimate (the 156-state votes prove the democratic basis exists).
- "Context-appropriate human judgement and control" formula — accept, **if** defined operationally (supervision, intervention, deactivation, temporal/spatial limits).
- Exclusions for defensive systems (C-RAM etc.) — negotiable, must be narrowly defined, non-expandable.

### Tradeables (give to get)
- Drop insistence on IHRL language *inside* core prohibitions (keep in preamble) → wins US-adjacent pragmatists like Singapore/UAE.
- Accept phased prohibitions (person-targeting ban first, review clause for expansion) → wins fence-sitters.
- Morocco offers to **co-host a regional capacity-building workshop on weapons reviews** (North Africa/African Group convening power) → buys African Group votes and praise.

## 5. VULNERABILITY REGISTER (see full defenses in file 10)

1. **"You operate Harops — a loitering munition with autonomous terminal homing."** → We regulate, we don't abstain from lawful tech; our doctrine targets *fully autonomous anti-personnel* engagement, not human-supervised munitions.
2. **Western Sahara drone allegations (Algeria, Nov 2021).** → Contested allegation; steer back to policy; Morocco complies with IHL and supports making that compliance *verifiable for everyone*.
3. **"You signed the US Political Declaration — so you accept soft law is enough."** → Wrong: we signed the stairs AND demand the door. The Declaration itself says principles must be implemented — binding law is the implementation.
4. **"Ban still hurts you — you import this technology."** → Two-tier protects lawful systems; Morocco fights smuggling/proliferation asymmetric threats.
5. **"2018 statement ≠ 2026 reality."** → Our votes in 2023, 2024, Vienna 2024, and continued African Group alignment prove continuity.
