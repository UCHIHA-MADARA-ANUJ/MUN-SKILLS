## 🎙️ GSL SPEECH — "THE PEN IS IN THIS ROOM" (90 seconds)

> **Main script · ~205 words · designed to land at 88–90s at measured pace.**

> Chair, distinguished delegates —
>
> In four weeks, the world's experts hold their final session in Geneva. In November, the Review Conference of the CCW decides whether machines will select human targets under law — or outside it. This committee is not observing history. It is holding the pen.
>
> Morocco comes with receipts, not adjectives. In 2013, we warned this organisation that no machine should hold the power of life and death. In 2018, we called for a binding instrument. We voted yes in 2023, yes in 2024 — and, distinguished delegates, Morocco operates advanced unmanned systems under human command every single day. We regulate this technology precisely because we understand it.
>
> Our answer to this agenda is the **Rabat Framework**: prohibit the unacceptable — autonomy against persons, and systems no human can predict — regulate the rest through reviews, testing, limits and logging, and write accountability into law, because machines cannot stand trial; humans can.
>
> The definition exists. The votes exist — one hundred sixty-six of them. What remains, delegates, is courage. The drafting table is open. Morocco is already standing at it. Thank you.

## 🎯 MOD SPEECH 1 — DEFINITIONS (60 seconds)

> **~135 words · 58–60s**

> A weapon does not check its own passport before it strikes, delegates. Definitions decide what the law reaches.
>
> Geneva's rolling text settled the spine twenty months ago: a system that can identify, select, and engage a target without human intervention. Morocco adds two upgrades. Cover distributed systems — a drone's brain may live on a server, and a cable is not a human controller. And drop the word "lethal" — lethality is an outcome, not a component.
>
> Delegates who cry "no definition" have tabled none in twelve years. Absence of text is not absence of technology.
>
> Write the spine into this committee's resolution, and the debate moves from vocabulary — to law.

**Why it wins:** you didn't complain about sabotage — you *closed* it and offered concrete text. Judges score the two upgrades as substance.

## ⚖️ MOD SPEECH 2 — LEGAL GAPS / ARTICLE 36 (60 seconds)

> **~125 words · 56–59s**

> "IHL suffices," they tell us. Sufficient for whom, delegates?
>
> Only about a dozen of 174 states are known to conduct Article 36 weapon reviews — and the United States, India, Türkiye, and Israel are not even bound by it. The Secretary-General's own report confirms: no dedicated instrument exists.
>
> Article 36 is a lock that fifteen states possess and the top developers never installed.
>
> Morocco's two-tier framework is the wall: prohibit anti-personnel and unpredictable systems — and obligate reviews, testing, bias checks, and lifecycle re-validation for everything else.
>
> Existing law commands a soldier to distinguish. Nothing on this earth tells a neural network how. We are here to write that sentence.

## 🤖 MOD SPEECH 3 — AI MISINTERPRETATION (60 seconds)

> **~125 words · 56–59s**

> In 2003, the finest military on Earth gave its operators ten seconds to veto a machine. The machine read a friendly aircraft as a missile. Three allied airmen died. That, delegates, is automation bias — humans rubber-stamping what the screen declares. Now scale it: reported industrial target queues processed in twenty-second reviews.
>
> This is not science fiction. It was last Wednesday.
>
> Morocco's answer is engineering law: minimum human judgement per attack, re-validation after every software update, bias testing, temporal and spatial limits, termination on anomaly. America's own Directive 3000.09 demands it at home.
>
> If it is law enough for the Pentagon, it is law enough for humanity. Sign it.

*(Note: "reported industrial target queues" = Lavender reference made attack-proof — reported, structural, no state named.)*

## 🕸️ MOD SPEECH 4 — ATTRIBUTION & SWARMS (60 seconds)

> **~130 words · 57–60s**

> In 2019, twenty-five drones and missiles struck the largest oil facility on Earth — and the world never legally settled who pulled the trigger. In June 2025, one hundred seventeen drones appeared from *inside* a border.
>
> Swarms launder responsibility — through proxies, through containers, through code. So Morocco tables the attribution chapter: tamper-evident operation logs on every system; chain-of-custody for targeting models; responsibility that follows the state that builds, fields, or transfers; and a UN technical roster able to examine evidence within thirty days.
>
> Delegates: a weapon that cannot be attributed cannot be deterred — and a law without attribution is a suggestion.
>
> Let us write the fingerprint into every swarm.

**This is Morocco's signature mod** — nobody else in the room owns attribution. Plant the flag.
