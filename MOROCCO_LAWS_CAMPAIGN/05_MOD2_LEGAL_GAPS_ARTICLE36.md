# 05 — MOD 2: EVALUATING LEGAL GAPS — IS EXISTING IHL + ARTICLE 36 ENOUGH?
**Morocco's answer: NO. And here is the proof stack, organized so no opponent escapes it.**

---

## THE OPPOSITION'S CASE (steel-manned — we destroy it fairly)

"IHL already fully applies to all weapons, including LAWS — the GGE's own 2019 Guiding Principles say so. Article 36 already obliges weapons reviews. New law is unnecessary, premature, and risks freezing innovation behind a definition nobody agrees on." — effectively the US/Russia/India/Israel line `[VERIFIED FACT as their position: Lawfare, Oct 2024; RCW reporting; CRS IF11150]`

Every clause of that is true — **except the conclusion.** Our job: show the four structural holes that no quantity of "IHL applies" can patch.

---

## HOLE 1 — THE ENFORCEMENT VOID: IHL HAS NO LAWS-SPECIFIC RULES, ONLY ANALOGIES

- **[VERIFIED FACT]** A/79/88 (2024): states noted *"there was no international legal instrument specifically regulating or prohibiting lethal autonomous weapons systems."*
- IHL rules (distinction, proportionality, precautions) were written for **human decision-makers**. Nobody has codified *what those rules require when the decider is a model*: How much situational understanding must the system have? Who performs the proportionality judgement and when? What does "feasible precautions" mean at machine speed?
- **Consequence:** identical conduct gets 193 different legal interpretations → **regulatory fragmentation** (A/79/88 ¶69 lists preventing fragmentation as a reason states want an LBI).
- **[ANALOGY]** Landmines and cluster munitions were also "already covered by IHL" — states still wrote dedicated treaties because general law failed to discipline a specific weapon class. Protocol IV blinding lasers banned *before battlefield death*, proving prevention is the CCW's own tradition.

**Line:** *"Saying IHL applies is like saying 'the rules of the road apply' to a car with no driver. Correct — and useless. The question the law has never answered is: who is steering, and how must the machine be built so that someone is?"*

## HOLE 2 — THE ARTICLE 36 ILLUSION: a review obligation almost nobody performs

| Fact | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Only **12–15 of 174 AP I parties** are known to have functioning weapon-review mechanisms (SIPRI, 2015); "fewer than 20" per ASPI 2019 | `[VERIFIED FACT]` | SIPRI Insight 2015/1; ASPI Strategist 2019 |
| Reviews are **purely national**: no standards, no methodology, no international oversight, no transparency duty | `[VERIFIED FACT]` | SIPRI 2015 |
| Key LAWS-developing states — **US, India, Pakistan, Türkiye, Israel — are NOT parties to AP I** and owe no Article 36 duty at all (US does reviews as a matter of *policy*, not treaty) | `[VERIFIED FACT]` | Lawfare, Oct 2024 |
| Reviews are binary legality checks ("is use prohibited in *some or all* circumstances?") — they were never designed to measure *degrees of human control*, algorithmic drift, or dataset bias | `[VERIFIED FACT]` | SIPRI 2015 |
| A weapon modified after review (software update!) may require re-review — with machine learning, the "weapon" changes *after fielding*, escaping the one-time review model | `[VERIFIED FACT]` | SIPRI 2015; ASP 2019 (ICRC: review is ongoing obligation) |

**Floor-kill:** *"Article 36 is a lock on a door that fifteen states possess, five of the top autonomy developers never installed, and the weapon can walk through after the inspection anyway. Delegates calling that 'sufficient' are confusing a lock with a wall."*

## HOLE 3 — THE ACCOUNTABILITY GAP: law binds humans, autonomy dissolves the chain

- **[VERIFIED FACT]** GGE rolling text Box V: *"IHL imposes obligations on States, parties to armed conflict and individuals, not on machines."* Everyone signed the principle — nobody has codified the mechanism.
- Who answers when an autonomous system misreads a wedding as a convoy? Operator (10-second veto window?), commander (dozens of simultaneous engagements?), programmer (wrote a model that *learned*?), procurer (tested it in a sandbox?) → the **accountability gap** documented across ICRC/academic work.
- International criminal law needs *mens rea*; a machine has none, and diffused human involvement dilutes provable intent. A/79/88 stresses accountability cannot be transferred — but **says no existing instrument operationalizes it for autonomy**.
- **Mod 4 link:** cross-border swarm → which state's conduct, which control standard? (file 07).

**Line:** *"Every delegation in Geneva has agreed that machines cannot bear responsibility. Beautiful. Now show me the article that tells a court which human bears it after a swarm crosses a border. It does not exist. That is the gap we are here to close."*

---

## HOLE 4 — THE ETHICS/ANTI-PERSONNEL GAP: IHL permits what conscience forbids

- **[VERIFIED FACT]** ICRC (12 May 2021): prohibit AWS designed/used to target persons + prohibit unpredictable systems + regulate all others — because compliance analysis alone doesn't settle the **Martens Clause** question (humanity & public conscience).
- **[VERIFIED FACT]** SG (A/79/88 + statements): autonomous targeting of humans is "a moral line that must not be crossed."
- IHL as written offers **no rule that a human must decide each death**. That is not a compliance question; it is a *law-absence* question — answered only by new law.
- **[VERIFIED FACT]** The GGE rolling text currently has **no anti-personnel prohibition** (SKR March 2026; Article 36 NGO) — proof that "let the CCW handle it under IHL" has so far *avoided the core moral issue*.

**Line:** *"Nothing in existing law forbids a machine from selecting a human being as a target. Nothing. If that sentence does not frighten this committee, what are we for?"*

## THE TWO-TIER ARCHITECTURE (Morocco's constructive answer — the alternative to "ban everything" AND "do nothing")

**Tier 1 — PROHIBIT:** systems whose effects cannot be sufficiently understood, predicted, explained (unpredictable/uncontrollable); systems designed or used to target persons (anti-personnel autonomy); systems operating without context-appropriate human judgement and control.

**Tier 2 — REGULATE everything else with positive obligations:** legal reviews *with* international transparency/exchange; testing & evaluation standards; limits on target types, duration, geographic scope, scale; human-machine interaction requirements (supervision, intervention, timely deactivation); lifecycle record-keeping for attribution (Mod 4 thread).

**Why it's FAMMS-proof:** F — anchored in the rolling text with >70 states ready; A — CCW/UNGA mandates documented; M — reviews, registries, reporting; M — capacity fund + shared review infrastructure (Morocco's Global South offer); S — sovereignty-preserving (transparency on methods, not classified secrets).
