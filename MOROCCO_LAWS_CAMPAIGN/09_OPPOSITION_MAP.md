## THEIR ARGUMENTS → OUR COUNTERS (rebuttal pairs)

### O1: "Existing IHL fully applies — new law is unnecessary."
**C1:** *"Application is not specification. IHL tells a soldier to distinguish; it does not tell a neural network how. Ninety-one states told the Secretary-General there is no dedicated instrument. If sufficiency were true, the CCW would not need a rolling text the length of a treaty skeleton."*

### O2: "A ban is unverifiable — autonomy is software."
**C2:** *"Landmines were 'unverifiable' until Ottawa created stockpile declarations and destruction deadlines. Verification is a design feature, not a reason to surrender. Our tiers regulate conduct + lifecycle documentation, which is exactly how software becomes auditable — logs, reviews, tested behaviour."*

### O3: "Definitions are immature — premature regulation is dangerous."
**C3:** *"A provisional consensus characterization has existed since November 2024. Producers of the delay now cite the delay as the reason for more delay. This committee should not applaud arsonists for complaining about smoke."* — then offer the fix: adopt the rolling-text spine + narrow exclusions.

### O4: "Autonomy protects civilians — precision reduces casualties" *(US 2018 white paper line)*
**C4:** *"Then codify it. If autonomy is so controllable, sign the control requirements: human judgement per attack, spatial-temporal limits, termination on anomaly. The refusal to write down what developers claim to practice is the confession."*

### O5: "Consensus process must be respected — UNGA bypass is illegitimate."
**C5:** *"Consensus built the rolling text — we honour that. But a working method is not a suicide pact: 166 states in 2024 and 156 in 2025 have pronounced the democratic will. If one consortium can veto humanity indefinitely, the legitimacy problem belongs to the veto, not to the majority."*

### O6: "Human control formulas are too vague to be legal obligations."
**C6:** *"So draft them precisely — that is what negotiations are for. The GGE already converged on context-appropriate human judgement and control. Your argument amounts to 'the text needs negotiation, therefore we refuse to negotiate.' Morocco invites you to the drafting table instead of the wrecking crew."*

### O7 (China-specific): "Only regulate USE, not development/production."
**C7 — build, don't blast:** *"Morocco hears the concern: production bans entrench first-mover advantages. Our two-tier design regulates capability classes, not national arsenals — with lifecycle obligations that bind developers and users equally. We invite the delegation to test its 'use-only' theory inside Tier 2 and see it strengthened, not sidelined."* (China voted WITH a binding-instrument statement in 2021 GGE — quote their own flexibility back.)

### O8 (Iran-sensitive / exporter-sensitive): "This is Western lawfare against our industries."
**C8:** *"Two states in our own bloc export drones. Morocco buys and operates them. This is not an embargo — it is a licence framework for a world where every militia can print a swarm. Producers gain legitimacy; victims gain law."*

- **Russia:** do not chase. Procedurally isolate: *"The majority will be heard in November; obstruction will be minuted."* Bank their no-votes as proof we need non-consensus fallback.
- **US:** court via the 3000.09 mirror argument (file 06) — they are a "not yet," not a "never." Best realistic outcome 2026: they don't block UNGA-space exploration + accept review/testing standards.
- **India:** invoke their peacekeeping + Global-South credentials; offer them leadership on **capacity-building for reviews** — flattery toward constructive role.
- **China:** targeted bilateral on the two-tier + defensive-system exclusions; their 2021 quote on negotiating a legal instrument after definitional consensus is our wedge — definitions now exist.
- **Türkiye:** exporter blame-deflection framing; Kargu-2 nuance is their vulnerability — invite them to co-draft *operator standards* so their systems get a compliance brand.
