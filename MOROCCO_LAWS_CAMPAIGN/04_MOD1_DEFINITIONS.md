# 04 — MOD 1: ESTABLISHING A WORKING DEFINITION OF AUTONOMOUS WEAPONS SYSTEMS
**Rule of this brief:** every claim labelled; every argument ends in a line you can say out loud.

---

## WHY THIS MOD IS A BATTLEFIELD (not a vocabulary exercise)

Definitions in arms control **decide what is regulated**. Whoever controls the definition controls the treaty. The blockers know it: a decade of "we cannot regulate what we cannot define" has been the perfect stall. Meanwhile a **provisional consensus characterization has existed in the GGE rolling text since Nov 2024** `[VERIFIED FACT]` — so "no agreed definition" is now an excuse, not a fact.

**Morocco's objective in this mod:** lock the room onto the GGE characterization, defeat definitional sabotage, and add two Moroccan precision upgrades (dispersed systems + lethality neutrality).

## MOROCCO'S TWO UPGRADES TO THE CONSENSUS TEXT `[NEGOTIATION STRATEGY]`

1. **Architecture clause:** characterization must expressly cover systems whose critical functions are *dispersed across networks* (drone + remote processor + munition), so a "weapon system" cannot evade regulation by outsourcing its brain to the cloud. (Article 36 NGO: "An integrated combination could imply all functions in one physical unit. This should be avoided." `[VERIFIED FACT]` they flagged exactly this.)
2. **Lethality neutrality:** drop "lethal" from the definitional spine; address lethality in the prohibition/regulation tiers, per ICRC. This collapses China's definitional sidetrack and widens the tent (captures anti-materiel swarms too — links Mod 4).

## THE POI PACK (Mod 1)

1. *"Does the delegate agree that a characterization has enjoyed provisional consensus in the GGE rolling text since November 2024 — and if so, which specific word in it does your delegation find unworkable, and why has it never tabled an alternative?"*
2. *"If your delegation insists on 'lethal' in the definition, would an identical system striking a vehicle convoy in one sortie and a refugee column in the next change legal categories mid-flight?"*
3. *"Your delegation speaks of definitions. When your Patriot-class defensive systems select and engage without intervention, should the treaty exclude them expressly — and will you negotiate the exclusion's edges, or just use it as a reason to block?"*
4. *"If a swarm's targeting intelligence lives on a server 800 kilometres from the munition, is it one weapon system under your definition — or zero?"*
