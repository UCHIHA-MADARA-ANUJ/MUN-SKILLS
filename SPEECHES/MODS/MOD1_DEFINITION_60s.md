## 1️⃣ THE SPEECH (memorize this)

Delegates, the United States armed forces have operated under a definition since 2012 — one sentence: a weapon that, once activated, can select and engage targets without further operator intervention. **[1]**

Geneva has held its own characterization by provisional consensus since November 2024 — **identify, select, and engage, without a human operator in the execution.** **[2]**

So when this committee is told we cannot regulate what we cannot define — delegates, that is no longer a fact. It is a **tactic**. And after twelve years, a tired one. **[3]**

Morocco proposes three moves. **Adopt** the consensus characterization as this committee's working definition — for the purposes of this resolution. **Keep it functional** — Geneva's text already reaches distributed systems; the brain need not sit inside the munition. **[4]** And fix the design principle that outlives every software update: **define the act, not the algorithm.**

The delegations crying "undefined" have tabled no text of their own in twelve years. **[5]** Twelve years spent defining. **Zero applying.** Morocco moves to reverse that ratio — today.

## 3️⃣ POI DEFENSE (mods get POIs — these are loaded)

1. **"Your definition is too broad — it captures autopilot, mines, and defensive CIWS that save lives."**
 → "Autopilot and mines follow human-set parameters and profiles; the characterization covers systems that *identify and select targets themselves* in execution. And if a defensive system is the concern — table a narrow, written exclusion, and we will negotiate its edges. Don't use the exception as a reason to block the rule."

2. **"Technology evolves — your definition is obsolete in two years."**
 → "That is precisely why we define by function. The American formulation has held since 2012; the Geneva text survived six sessions of engineering review. Hardware lists expire. **The act doesn't.**"

3. **"A human setting target parameters escapes your definition — it has a hole."**
 → "Read the Geneva clarifiers: a human setting parameters does **not** exempt the system from characterization. The hole is already patched — in text."

4. **"Why not simply adopt the American wording?"**
 → "Because Geneva's text is owned by every party to the CCW and refined by six sessions — including the 'functionally integrated' language that stops a system outsourcing its brain to a server. We borrow everywhere; we adopt consensus."

5. **"Naming these weapons legitimizes them."**
 → "We named chemical agents in order to prohibit them. A definition is the lock on the door, not the key. You cannot regulate what you refuse to name — that was true in 2014; what changed is that the name now exists."

6. **"Which word of the consensus text is unworkable, then — and why has no alternative been tabled?"** *(best-case POI — fire it back if an ally doesn't)*
 → "Exactly. Twelve years in, no critic has produced a better text. Morocco invites any delegation with drafting language to the working group — but we will not outsource the calendar to delegations that table nothing."
