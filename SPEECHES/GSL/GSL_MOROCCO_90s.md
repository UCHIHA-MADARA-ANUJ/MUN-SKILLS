## THE SPEECH

<!-- SPEECH-START -->
Honourable Chair, esteemed judges, distinguished delegates —

*March 2020. Libya. African soil.* A retreating convoy, hunted by a **Kargu-2** needing no human signal — **fire, forget, and find.** The first **reported** autonomous engagement. **No confirmed dead. No one named. No law.**
[PAUSE]

**The report this Assembly requested — A-79/88 — records it: no instrument specifically regulates these weapons.** One hundred seventy-five states must review new ones; **twelve to fifteen are known to do it; not one must publish.** Geneva's rolling text from December already writes the principle: *"responsibility and accountability cannot be transferred to machines."* **The words exist. They bind no one.**

Our agenda's first word is ***"evaluating"* — not "postponing."** **Answer three questions in your next speech, delegates:** the rule that guarantees meaningful human control; the standard that defines how much control is enough; the mechanism that names who answers when it fails. **Twelve years. No names.**

**Some will rise after me calling this premature** — while their own doctrine demands human judgment over the use of force. *Ask them which they plan to abandon.* **This Assembly acted in 2023, in 2024, and December — the no column grew: four, three, six. *We are still speaking.***

*Morocco demands nothing it will not do.* Morocco flies these systems under human command — and our clauses bind our fleet: **control that cannot be withdrawn, reviews that cannot grade themselves, records that cannot disappear.**

**If these safeguards already exist, codify them. If they do not, this committee must. The principle is one sentence long: the decision to use lethal force must remain under meaningful human control.**
[PAUSE]

***Morocco has chosen. Add your name — or be named by your absence.***

## POI DEFENSE — eight executions + the dagger

**Q1. "STM denies the Kargu-2 flew autonomously. You named a brand recklessly."**
I named what the UN Panel of Experts named: report S/2021/229 records the hunt and a capability it called "fire, forget and find." I said *reported*, I confirmed no dead. The delegate argues with the UN's own record, not with me.

**Q2. "No confirmed dead — so your opening is a non-event."**
That *is* the case. The first reported autonomous engagement left nothing countable — no death confirmed, no owner named, no law required — because no law required any of it. **The danger is not that we cannot prove the machine killed. It is that if it did, no one would ever have to tell us.**

**Q3. "A-79/88 merely records states' views — not conclusions."**
Exactly why I said "records." Paragraph 21 records what no capital disputes: no instrument specifically regulates these weapons. Paragraph 38 records that no state must publish a review. Paragraph 37 records that reviews alone are insufficient. I cite paragraph numbers, delegate. The podium next to you cited adjectives.

**Q4. "Who is the 'premature' camp? Whose doctrine? Name them."**
The record names itself. Geneva, March of this year: one delegation tried to strike the words "human control" and replace them with "good faith human judgment and care." The room refused. That same capital's own defence directive demands appropriate human judgment over the use of force — and its flag sat in the no column in December. My speech touches no placard. The journal touches all of them.

**Q5. "Your own numbers betray you — the no column GREW from three to six."**
Because the deadline is real. Obstruction now needs six flags to do what three once did — against one hundred sixty-four. When the majority is twenty-seven to one and still told it must wait, the problem is not arithmetic. It is nerve. This committee has both.

**Q6. "Morocco flies strike drones. Sit down."**
Under human command — precisely the point. We operate this technology and wrote clauses that bind us first: control that cannot be withdrawn, reviews that cannot grade themselves, records that cannot disappear. A state that flies these systems and asks to be bound is harder to dismiss than one that lectures from safety.

**Q7. "Your December vote column is a committee vote, not the Assembly's."**
The First Committee is this Assembly's own disarmament arm — 164 to 6, on 1 December 2025 — following the Assembly's own recorded votes of 2023 and 2024. Three years, three mandates, one direction. I said "this Assembly acted." It did — through the organ it built for exactly this.

**Q8. "If Geneva's text already writes the principle, your campaign is redundant."**
The text proves the principle is *ready* — not that it is *binding*. That gap is our entire clause set: control, review, record. By March, over seventy states were reported ready to negotiate on that text. The words exist. They bind no one. We are the difference between them.

**ALGERIA DAGGER:** *"Morocco binds its own fleet in its own text — no fence around ourselves. Test us against our clauses."* One line. Never bilateral. Sit.
