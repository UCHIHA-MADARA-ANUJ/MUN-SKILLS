## THE SPEECH

<!-- SPEECH-START -->
Honourable Chair, esteemed delegates — **the first reported autonomous engagement already sits on the United Nations record: a weapon designed to fire, forget, and find.** No rule required a human to decide. **No name has answered.**

Even Washington's own directive on autonomous weapons requires **"appropriate levels of human judgment over the use of force."** If restraint is doctrine at home, why is it **premature** as law?

Three states voted no on the last resolution — against talk itself: **Belarus. The DPRK. Russia.**

Delegations insist existing law suffices. Then three questions: **the rule that guarantees meaningful human control; the standard that defines how much control is enough; the mechanism that identifies who answers when that control fails.** [PAUSE] Twelve years. No names. "Sufficient" is not an answer — it is a **bet** that the question will never be asked.

Morocco does not oppose this technology; **we operate unmanned systems under human command.** Three commitments, in the text twenty-nine delegations sponsor: **binding meaningful human control, because a safeguard that can be withdrawn is no safeguard; weapons reviews against shared criteria, because self-assessment is not oversight; recorded engagements, because accountability without a record is an assertion, not a mechanism.**

**If these safeguards already exist, codify them. If they do not, this committee must.** **The principle is one sentence long: the decision to use lethal force must remain under meaningful human control.** [PAUSE]

Morocco has chosen. Who is with us?
<!-- SPEECH-END -->

## POI DEFENSE — post-speech fire, six executions + the dagger

**Q1. "The Libya case was never proven autonomous — even casualties are unconfirmed. Why build on an unproven event?"**
I built on what the record holds: a UN Panel of Experts reported lethal autonomous weapons hunting down and engaging retreating forces — the first *reported* autonomous engagement, unresolved. The manufacturer disputes it; that is why the word "reported" exists. And notice what nobody in this room disputes: no rule required a human decision that day. None does today. The case proves the gap even in its weakest reading — that is why it opens this speech.

**Q2. "Why name Belarus, the DPRK and Russia? Naming states is hardly diplomatic."**
I named a public vote, not a personality. The Assembly's voting record is a document, not an insult — 166 voted to keep talking; three voted against talk itself. If hearing one's own vote read aloud stings, the wound is self-inflicted.

**Q3. "The US directive you quote proves flexibility works — why freeze it into law?"**
Because "appropriate" is doing all the work in that sentence and no international instrument defines it. A dial every state sets privately is not a floor; it is a ceiling no one else can see. We are not freezing one model — we are pouring the foundation under all of them.

**Q4. "Twelve years is theatrical — serious negotiation began far later."**
Count from the first dedicated CCW meeting, 2014: twelve. From the Human Rights Council debate: thirteen. Choose the delegate's favourite calendar — every version ends in the same inventory: no instrument, no standard, no published determination. Argue over the birthday; the silence on the record is unambiguous.

**Q5. "Morocco buys drones. What standing does an importer have to write the rules?"**
Operator standing. We field these systems under human command — and our own clauses bind what we fly: review against shared criteria, human capacity to intervene, recorded engagements. The delegations holding only theory write fear. Operators write rules that survive contact. Twenty-nine sponsors behind the text agree.

**Q6. "Answer plainly: does Morocco want a ban or not?"**
Read the architecture. Systems a human cannot control, systems that kill humans without a human decision, systems no one can predict or explain — prohibited. Everything else — lawful, under binding conditions. We oppose no technology. We oppose a silence in the law exactly where a name should be.

**ALGERIA DAGGER:** *"Every clause Morocco sponsors binds Morocco first — we wrote no fence around ourselves. The floor is welcome to test us against our own text."* One line. Never go bilateral. Sit.
