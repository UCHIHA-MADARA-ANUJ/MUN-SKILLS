## THE SPEECH

<!-- SPEECH-START -->
Honourable Chair, esteemed judges, distinguished delegates —

*March 2020. Libya. African soil.* A retreating convoy, hunted by a **Kargu-2** needing no human signal — **fire, forget, and find.** The first **reported** autonomous engagement. **No confirmed dead. No one named. No law.**
*On any other soil, this treaty would already exist.*
[PAUSE]

One hundred seventy-five states must review new weapons — **twelve to fifteen do it. None must show the result.** Yet they insist law suffices.

**Answer three questions in your next speech, delegates:** the rule that guarantees meaningful human control; the standard that defines how much control is enough; the mechanism that names who answers when that control fails. **Twelve years. No names.** "Sufficient" is a bet the question never gets asked.

**Some call binding law premature — while their own doctrine demands human judgment over every use of force.** *Ask them which one they plan to abandon.* **Some would ban the use — never the making.** A fence at the battlefield; nothing at the factory. **Three delegations voted against this Assembly even speaking. *We are speaking.***

*Morocco demands nothing it will not do.* We fly unmanned systems under human command — and our text binds our own fleet: **control that cannot be withdrawn, reviews that cannot grade themselves, records that cannot disappear. If we bind ourselves, so can you.**

**Geneva answers to this Assembly — send it instructions, not excuses.**

**If these safeguards already exist, codify them. If they do not, this committee must. The principle is one sentence long: the decision to use lethal force must remain under meaningful human control.**
[PAUSE]

***Morocco has chosen. Add your name — or be named by your absence.***
<!-- SPEECH-END -->

## POI DEFENSE — seven executions + the dagger

**Q1. "STM denies the Kargu-2 operated autonomously. You named a brand recklessly."**
I named what the United Nations Panel of Experts named: report S/2021/229 records a weapon that hunted and a capability it called "fire, forget and find." I said *reported*. I said *unresolved*. The delegate argues with the UN's own record, not with me.

**Q2. "No confirmed dead — so your opening is a non-event."**
That is my case. The first reported autonomous engagement left nothing countable — no death confirmed, no name attached, no law required — because no law required any of it. **The danger is not that we cannot prove the machine killed. It is that if it did, no one would ever have to tell us.**

**Q3. "'On any other soil' — is the delegate accusing this room of racism?"**
The delegate heard a comparison of urgency, because one exists. Six years after Libya there is still no instrument. My line was a counterfactual — and it comes with a standing offer: **name the treaty that followed that convoy, and I withdraw the sentence on the spot.**

**Q4. The triple name-trap: "Which three? Whose doctrine? Who bans use-not-making?"**
The record is public, and the speech never touched a placard. **Three:** the negative votes on Resolution 79/62 — Belarus, the DPRK, the Russian Federation. **Doctrine:** any state whose own defence directive demands "appropriate levels of human judgment over the use of force" while calling this file premature. **Use-not-making:** the position on record at the CCW in 2018 — ban the use, license the factory. Delegations recognize themselves. That is the design.

**Q5. "The CCW answers to no one — it is a consensus body, not your subordinate."**
And it reports every year to this Assembly — the same First Committee that put these weapons on the agenda, requested the Secretary-General's report, and voted 166 to 3 to keep this file alive. The Assembly drives; Geneva delivers. Its final session under the current mandate meets days after we rise. Instructions are what it is waiting for.

**Q6. "Morocco flies Turkish strike drones. Sit down."**
Under human command — which is precisely the point. Morocco operates this technology and still wrote rules that bind us first: control that cannot be withdrawn, reviews that cannot grade themselves, records that cannot disappear. A state that flies these systems and asks to be bound is harder to dismiss than one that lectures from safety. If we bind ourselves, so can you.

**Q7. "'Be named by your absence' — theatrics to close a speech."**
It is a description of the roll call. Every vote in this room is recorded, and every silence is a position with a flag on it. I did not threaten the room. I read the room its own minutes.

**ALGERIA DAGGER:** *"Morocco binds its own fleet in its own text — no fence around ourselves. Test us against our clauses."* One line. Never bilateral. Sit.
