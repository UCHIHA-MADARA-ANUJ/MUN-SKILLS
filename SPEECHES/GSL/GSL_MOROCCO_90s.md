## THE SPEECH

<!-- SPEECH-START -->
Honourable Chair, esteemed judges, distinguished delegates —

*March 2020. Libya. African soil.* A retreating convoy, hunted by a **Kargu-2** that needed no human signal — **fire, forget, and find.** The United Nations recorded the first reported autonomous engagement. The casualties: unconfirmed. The responsible: unnamed. The law governing that hunt: **none.**
[PAUSE]

One hundred seventy-five states must review new weapons — **twelve to fifteen do it. None must show the result.**

Yet they insist: **existing law suffices.** Then answer three questions in your next speech, delegates: **the rule that guarantees meaningful human control; the standard that defines how much control is enough; the mechanism that identifies who answers when that control fails.** Twelve years. **No names.** "Sufficient" is a bet the question never gets asked.

*Morocco does not bet.* We fly unmanned systems under human command — and bind our own fleet to our text: **binding meaningful human control, because a safeguard that can be withdrawn is no safeguard; reviews on shared criteria, because self-assessment is not oversight; recorded engagements, because accountability without a record is an assertion, not a mechanism.**

The Secretary-General and the Red Cross asked: **finish by 2026. It is 2026.** Days after we rise, Geneva holds its final scheduled session. We send a position — **or an excuse.**

**If these safeguards already exist, codify them. If they do not, this committee must. The principle is one sentence long: the decision to use lethal force must remain under meaningful human control.**
[PAUSE]

***History is written by the rooms that answer.*** **Morocco has chosen to be that room. *Add your name.***
<!-- SPEECH-END -->

## POI DEFENSE — six executions + the dagger

**Q1. "STM denies the Kargu-2 operated autonomously. You named a brand recklessly."**
I named what the United Nations Panel of Experts named: report S/2021/229 records a weapon that hunted and a capability it called "fire, forget and find." I said *reported*. I said *unresolved*. The delegate is arguing with his own record, not mine.

**Q2. "Casualties were unconfirmed — so your big story is a non-event."**
That sentence is my case. History's first reported autonomous engagement left nothing countable — no casualty figure, no review, no responsible name — because no law required any of them. The danger is not that we cannot prove the machine killed. **The danger is that if it did, no one would ever have to tell us.**

**Q3. "Who exactly calls law 'premature'? Stop hinting."**
The United States mission in Geneva — 2017, 2018, and their State Department again in 2023 — all on record, while their own defence directive demands appropriate levels of human judgment. I quote governments on the floor; I never touch placards. The camp heard itself long before you asked.

**Q4. "Three delegations said no to what, precisely?"**
Resolution 79/62, December 2024 — passed 166–3–15. The three negative votes are public: Belarus, the DPRK, the Russian Federation. The journal named them; my speech never needed to.

**Q5. "You taunted the room to 'answer in your next speech.' This is arrogance, not debating."**
Interrogation is the floor's function, delegate. If any speaker answers the three questions — the rule, the standard, the name — Morocco will be their first co-sponsor. The three questions have been open for twelve years. My invitation to close them stands to everyone equally.

**Q6. "Poetry at the end — 'history is written by rooms' — where is the substance?"**
The substance preceded it: three binding conditions in the text before you — human control, reviews on shared criteria, recorded engagements. The final sentence was not decoration; it was a deadline. Geneva's last scheduled session starts one week after this room rises.

**ALGERIA DAGGER:** *"Morocco binds its own fleet in its own text — no fence around ourselves. Test us against our clauses."* One line. Never bilateral. Sit.
